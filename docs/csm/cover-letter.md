## How to use this

**Rewrite the hook every time.** It is the only paragraph a hiring manager reads closely,
and a generic one is worse than no letter. If you cannot name something specific about the
company, you have not researched enough to apply yet.

**The middle two paragraphs are your reusable core.** They carry the positioning from
`jd-analysis.md`: the SaaS operator who *built* the system, not the account babysitter.
Do not dilute this into "I am passionate about customer success" — every other applicant
has written that sentence.

**Insert one number as soon as you have it.** The moment you can write "held X% retention
across N enterprise accounts", put it in the second paragraph. It changes the letter more
than any rewording will.

**Address a person.** "Dear Hiring Team" is a last resort. Check the JD, the company's
LinkedIn page, and the team page. It takes four minutes and materially lifts reply rates.
