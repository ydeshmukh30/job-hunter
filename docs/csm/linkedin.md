# LinkedIn optimisation — Customer Success Manager

Recruiters search LinkedIn with the same strings an ATS parses. The current profile is
strong on substance and weak on findability — these changes are cheap and compounding.

## Headline

**Current:** `CBR @ Superset | MBA PM @ UCAM`

The problem: "CBR" is internal shorthand no recruiter searches for, and "MBA PM" is
ambiguous — it reads as Product Management to half the people who see it. Neither the
words "Customer Success" nor "Enterprise" appear, so the profile is invisible to the exact
search a CSM recruiter runs.

**Recommended:**

```
Manager, Enterprise Customer Success @ Superset | SaaS Retention, Onboarding & Adoption | MBA
```

**Alternative, if you want the metric forward** — use once you have the retention number:

```
Enterprise Customer Success @ Superset | [X]% retention across [N] accounts | SaaS onboarding & adoption | MBA
```

Both keep you under the 220-character limit and put "Customer Success", "Enterprise",
"SaaS", "retention", "onboarding" and "adoption" into the indexed field.

## About section

The profile currently has no About section. This is the largest single miss — it is fully
indexed, it is the first thing a recruiter reads, and an empty one reads as an inactive
profile. Draft:

---

I manage enterprise customer success at Superset, where our customers are universities and
employers running hiring cycles that cannot be rescheduled. When an onboarding slips, a
recruitment season slips with it. That has made me unusually rigorous about time-to-value.

Over 5+ years across SaaS companies in India, Singapore and Australia I have worked most
of the customer lifecycle: 500+ international relationships at WhiteHat Jr, APAC
client-facing operations at Multiplier, and — as a founding team member at saasguru — the
customer training and operations function built from nothing across three regions.

That founding experience is the thread. I have written the SOPs, built the onboarding
bootcamps and set the review cadence rather than inheriting someone else's. It taught me
that most churn is decided in the first thirty days, and that the customers who are about
to leave are usually the ones who have stopped complaining.

I hold an MBA in Project Management from UCAM (Spain), 9/10.

Currently `[[NEED: open to opportunities? specify what you want — segment, industry,
location, remote]]`. Reach me at `[[NEED: email]]`.

## Other changes, in order of payoff

1. **Turn on "Open to work"** (recruiters-only visibility if you are still at Superset).
   Single biggest lever on inbound.
2. **Rename the Multiplier entry.** "COE/CS" means nothing outside the company. Use
   "Customer Success / Centre of Excellence" — as in `resume.md`.
3. **Reorder your skills.** Of 51 listed, the surfaced ones are Project Support and
   Administrative Management — both of which read *junior* and neither of which is on the
   JD. Pin these three instead: **Customer Success**, **Account Management**,
   **Customer Retention**.
4. **Add tool skills** once confirmed: CRM, CS platform, support desk. Recruiters filter
   on these explicitly.
5. **Get endorsements on the pinned three.** Ask two colleagues. Takes them one click.
6. **Keep the career break entry.** It is honest, it is explained, and "took time to
   complete my MBA" is a good reason. Do not delete it — an unexplained gap is worse.
7. **Post occasionally about customer success**, not only Superset reposts. Your current
   activity is all company reshares, which reads as marketing rather than practitioner.
   Three posts on onboarding or retention would change how the profile reads.
