## Role summary

A customer success manager (CSM) ensures that clients receive the complete value of a
company's products or services, fostering long-term relationships and driving customer
satisfaction. The role focuses on client retention and satisfaction rather than
sales-driven revenue generation.

## Responsibilities

- Managing and growing relationships with key customers as their primary contact
- Engaging proactively to understand customer goals and challenges
- Ensuring high retention and satisfaction through exceptional service
- Gathering feedback and communicating product improvements to development
- Collaborating with internal teams to resolve issues and deliver products/services
- Developing customer success strategies that drive adoption and maximize value
- Monitoring health metrics, tracking KPIs, and reporting outcomes to leadership
- Advocating for customer needs within the organization
- Onboarding new clients and guiding product setup and training
- Conducting regular check-ins and business reviews
- Identifying upselling and cross-selling opportunities
- Managing customer lifecycle and escalations
- Creating customized reports and delivering training sessions

## Required qualifications

- Bachelor's degree in Business, Marketing, Communications, or related field
- 3+ years as customer success manager, account manager, or related role
- Experience managing customer success programs and retention strategies
- Ability to analyze customer data and usage trends
- Understanding of product management's impact on customer experience
- Strong interpersonal and communication skills
- Excellent problem-solving and critical-thinking abilities
- Strong organizational and time management skills
- Familiarity with CRM tools and customer success platforms
- Solid negotiation skills for complex accounts

## Preferred qualifications

- Advanced degree in Business, Marketing, or related field
- Relevant certifications (SuccessHACKER, CSM certification)
- SaaS, technology, or B2B industry experience
- Knowledge of customer success frameworks and best practices
- Experience with tools like Gainsight, ChurnZero, or HubSpot
- Data analytics and reporting experience
- Enterprise-level account management experience
- Conflict resolution proficiency
- Multilingual abilities in regional languages
